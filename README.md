# GE-Player
An audio player in the browser! With this PWA, you can play any audio file all in your browser! No download required! No more downloading from suspicious websites, no SmartScreen or Gatekeeper popups, no complex installations, no malware, no faff! Just upload an audio file, click play and enjoy! And the best part? None of your files are uploaded to any server! They stay on your computer! Privacy is part of our “etiquette”, you know. Not just gaming!

[Go to the app!](https://www.ge-player.edgeone.dev)
